# Penumbra design system

This document records the interface decisions that govern the dashboard in
[`docs/index.html`](index.html). The source of truth is
[`dashboard/styles.css`](../dashboard/styles.css), [`dashboard/app.js`](../dashboard/app.js),
and [`dashboard/template.html`](../dashboard/template.html).

## Identity

Penumbra is a solar eclipse detection lab. The page is a night observatory that
opens into a paper record: a full-bleed dark hero with a live starfield and a
phase-responsive sun/moon, then clean paper plates for the evidence. It should
feel authored by an observer, not generated by a component library.

The mark is a black disc with a coral upper corona, an amber lower arc, and four
registration ticks. At favicon size it reads as an eclipse; at rail size it reads
as the same instrument as the cover plate.

The voice is direct and evidentiary. Eclipse language names the chapters
(Penumbra, First light, Instruments, Umbra, Corona, Eclipses, Recurrence), but
the copy still says exactly what the pipeline does.

## Principles

1. **Evidence before decoration.** Every number traces to an event, a detection,
   or a coverage rule. The eclipse is a frame for the data, never a substitute.
2. **One showpiece per act.** The night stage, the light curve, the corona ring,
   the case reel, and the terminal each own one moment. The rest stays quiet.
3. **Corona and umbra are one system.** The Corona theme is the record in
   daylight; the Umbra theme is the same record under a black sky. The run ticket
   stays paper in both.
4. **Motion is an instrument.** The corona turns, the scope sweeps, the chart
   draws, and the ring breathes. Everything pauses under reduced motion.
5. **The dashboard is the deliverable.** One self-contained HTML file, no CDN,
   no framework, no build step, no external font request.

### Roles

- **Coral** is the primary accent: the cover word, chapter indices, labels,
  primary actions, active tabs, and the eclipse marker.
- **Sunset ramp** is the corona: the cover plate, the scope trace, the corona
  ring, the chart envelope, and the footer rule.
- **Black / totality** is the instrument and the end band. The plate, the scope,
  the rail, the terminal, and the footer share it.
- **Paper and stone** carry the record. Cards, tables, and the matrix sit on
  paper; the stone scale carries secondary surfaces and rules.
- **Severity** keeps four distinct steps: coral for critical, ember for high,
  amber for medium, stone for low. Every step also has a text label.
- **No color carries meaning alone.** Severity, coverage, and status always
  print a word or a count beside the color.

## Typography

- **Display: Fraunces** 400-500, roman and italic. The cover lyric, chapter
  titles, case titles, the coverage numeral, the footer wordmark, and the big
  chapter indices use it. Its high contrast is the eclipse-poster voice.
- **Poster: Manrope 800.** One word per chapter title and the `SHADOW.` line on
  the cover. It is the loud counterweight to Fraunces.
- **Data: JetBrains Mono 400, 500, and 600.** Labels, timestamps, IDs, table values, and
  commands. Mono is never body copy.
- **Scale:** 8, 9, 9.5, 10.5, 12, 14, 15, 16, 19, 22, 30, 36, 46, 66, 100, 122.
- **Line length:** ledes cap at 54-58ch; chapter ledes at 58ch; matrix labels
  wrap inside their tiles.
- **Numbers** use `tabular-nums` in tables, tickets, and chart axis text.
- **All-caps labels** are letterspaced 0.12-0.2em and kept to one line where the
  layout allows.

## Layout

- **Cover.** A full-bleed night stage: a canvas starfield, a giant ghost
  `PENUMBRA`, a real sun + moon disc that shifts with the selected contact, a
  paper run ticket, and a kinetic phase ticker at the boundary. The lyric and
  actions sit on the night field. Under 1080px the columns stack.
- **Interlude.** The ticker hands off to a paper band with a soft corona arc, a
  black path-of-totality plate, a five-node contact path, and a Baily's-beads
  rule. In Umbra the band goes dark and the path plate goes paper.
- **Totality act.** A 240vh pinned sequence between the ticker and the paper
  interlude. Native scroll drives the moon across the sun, the corona, Baily's
  beads, the diamond ring, the phase word, and a progress rail. Reduced motion
  renders the static totality frame.
- **Chapters.** A serif index, a one-word label, a serif title with one Manrope
  word, and a 58ch lede. The rule above each chapter is stone, with a small bead
  cluster at the right edge.
- **Instruments.** The scope is a full-width black plate with a warm light curve.
  The chart is a black instrument with a warm envelope, four severity stems,
  a gold grid, and a dark readout.
- **Corona.** A 300px radial dial with 45 ticks, one per priority technique.
  Coral, ember, and amber fill the four quadrants; the score sits in the eye.
- **Cases.** A chain player above a horizontal case reel. The reel uses native
  scroll-snap plus previous/next controls; each card has a full-width sunset
  top rule, a serif title, a faint case index, and a corona ring.
- **Footer.** A black totality band with a serif wordmark, chapter links, and one
  coral dot. A four-stop sunset rule sits on its top edge.

## Eclipse geometry

- **Sky.** A fixed corona ring, sunburst, dark-moon watermark, and celestial
  dust sit behind the sheet at low opacity. They read as the sky, not as
  content. The paper fold, grain, and edge vignette are gone.
- **Plates.** Cards use a 2px/24px cut corner. Buttons, chips, inputs, tabs,
  and the toast are capsules. Coverage tiles use a 1px/10px cut.
- **Nodes.** Method stages, case-chain nodes, severity marks, and phase marks
  are eclipse discs or crescents. The active method stage carries a corona ring
  and a pulse; the active phase mark pings from the contact ribbon.
- **Lines.** Section rules carry a Baily's-beads stitch. The contact ribbon and
  the path of totality draw the five contacts as one instrument. The rail
  progress ends in a small moon, and the case-chain progress is a transform line
  behind the nodes.
- **Controls.** The contact ribbon is interactive: each contact pins the matching
  hour, and `P` plays the whole C1-C4 sequence without scrolling. Buttons share
  one hierarchy: a sunset-gradient primary, an ink secondary in Corona (dark
  with a paper edge in Umbra), and a transparent ghost. Chip, field, and mini
  controls use the same capsule and mono label.
- **Gradients.** The sun ramp appears in the cover, ticket, scope, chart,
  interlude, dial halo, case rings, terminal, palette, toast, and footer.
  Gradients are surfaces and instruments, never body text.

## Motion

| Element | Motion |
|---|---|
| Cover lines | Masked rise, 1s `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` |
| Cover plate | Clip-path reveal, 1s, 0.2s delay |
| Hero starfield | 30fps twinkle, pointer parallax, rare shooting star |
| Sun / moon | Moon shifts with the selected contact; corona dims off totality |
| Ghost wordmark | Scroll lag, 0.14× |
| Phase ticker | 36s linear loop; pauses on hover |
| Case reel | Native scroll-snap; smooth previous/next |
| Totality act | 240vh scroll; moon position, corona, beads, diamond, copy, progress |
| Coverage ring | ±9° scroll-linked turn |
| Corona disc | 44s linear rotation |
| Corona streamers | 68s counter-rotation |
| Diamond ring | 4.2s pulse |
| Path of totality | Interactive: contacts and the data-peak marker open the hour in the watch floor |
| Phase ribbon | Reveal with the cover; `P` plays C1-C4 |
| Ticket stamp | 460ms press to a 2.5-degree settle |
| Scope | 9s scan column; pings on loud hours |
| Chart | 8s replay while visible; envelope draws in |
| Corona ring | 7s breathe, 42ms per tick |
| Sky sunburst | 150s rotation |
| Baily's beads | 16s shimmer |
| Method node | 9s orbit, 3s ring pulse |
| Rail moon | Follows the scroll fill |
| Theme switch | 620ms circular eclipse wipe |
| Case chain | 1.2s per step; progress `scaleX`; active dot pings |

Reduced motion keeps every final state: the plate is static, the corona does not
turn, the sky and beads do not move, the ring does not breathe, the theme
switch is instant, the chain does not autoplay, and the scope draws once.

## Instruments

- **Live scope.** A canvas light curve on a black plate. The trace is a horizontal
  sunset gradient; the peak is a black disc with a coral ring, an amber halo, and
  a diamond flare. It pauses offscreen and re-draws on theme change.
- **Fig. 0.** The path of totality: a black plate inside the gradient interlude,
  a five-node contact path, a data-peak marker placed from the actual peak hour,
  and a caption that reports the peak. Selecting a contact or the peak marker
  opens that hour in the watch floor.
- **Fig. 1.** An SVG chart of matched events by hour. The envelope is ember; the
  stems are critical/high/medium/low. Hover, keyboard arrows, and click pin an
  hour.
- **Corona ring.** 45 radial ticks generated from the coverage payload. Each tick
  names its technique and the rule or correlation that closes it.
- **Contact ribbon.** C1, C2, totality, C3, C4 with times, above a ticket block
  that adds Saros 145, magnitude 1.02, and 100% obscuration.
- **Run ticket.** The live numbers (events, rules, correlations, errors) sit on a
  paper receipt with a barcode and a verified stamp.

## Accessibility

- Skip link to the watch floor.
- Every icon-only control has an `aria-label`; every status has a word.
- Focus is a 2px coral outline with a 3px offset.
- Watch floor rows support `J`/`K` and `Enter`; `/` focuses search; `T` switches
  theme; `P` plays the eclipse; `Ctrl K` opens the command palette.
- Touch targets are at least 24px; the primary controls are 44px.
- The page passes WCAG AA contrast for text pairs in both themes.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the corona rotation, the ring breathe, the
  cover entrance, and the chart replay.

## Verification

- `python -m pipeline.report` rebuilds `docs/index.html` from the template,
  styles, script, icon, fonts, and payload.
- `python -m pipeline.heatmap` rebuilds the ATT&CK SVG.
- `python -m pipeline.lint`, `compile --check`, `coverage --check`, the heatmap
  diff, and `pytest` are the release gate.
- Lighthouse is run on the built page at desktop width; the target is 100 in
  accessibility, best practices, and SEO, with no console errors.
- Screenshots are checked at 1440 and at a true 390px iframe, in both themes.
